Overview · The recommended bundle

Ship the honest bundle, then earn the identity.

Four decisions, one path. Wave 1 fixes what blocks trust and the finish line; Wave 2 pays down the structural debt; the visual identity waits until the product has a positioning to express. Nothing here is a rebrand, and every claim is true on the first screen.

WHY THIS IS THE LONG-RUN CHOICE
عقد لغوي واحد
One scoped privacy sentence, one error taxonomy and one copy standard — reused by onboarding, support, and future surfaces.
عربية أصلية لا مُصلَّحة
Nav, type and tracking derived from Arabic first; the rebuilt layer becomes the QA baseline for every locale.
إعادة استخدام بلا وعد جديد
Copy-first is the primitive for share, publish and MCP later; no new architecture is needed to expose it.
هوية مؤجلة لا ملغاة
The echo motif is already structurally present in 3.10; 4.0 amplifies it instead of restarting from zero.
THE ONE THING TO PROTECT

Do not spend the identity budget before the trust sentence is true and the finish line is one click. A prettier shell cannot fix a promise the second screen breaks.
