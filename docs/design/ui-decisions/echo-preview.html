3 · Motif

Continuity footline — only the centre bar carries the signal colour, bars are flat and 2 px:

Three uses only (brief §2): wordmark, empty-state rings behind the drop target clipped at one edge, and a 10-bar waveform footline. Never a spinner, never a background pattern, never a decorative border.

7 · What to review

  • Temperature. Is paper calmer than the current chat-app neutrals, or too warm?
  • Signal discipline. Terracotta as one accent per screen — signal, not theme?
  • Ink rail. Right direction for the eventual rail restyle next to paper?
  • Arabic reading. Body 14–15 / 1.7 on paper comfortable at arm's length?
  • Motif. Rings + footline worth building the shell around?
  • Trust. Does anything weaken the literal privacy line? It must stay its own slot.

Report the exact screen and problem. Components the brief did not audit may have weak pairs in a token-only pass — that is expected. Dark theme is out of scope: preview in light mode.
